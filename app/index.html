America by the Numbers is an independent statistical atlas. Every figure is a published statistic of the United States federal statistical system — the Census Bureau, the Bureau of Economic Analysis, the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the National Center for Health Statistics and the National Center for Education Statistics — and each chart and table names its source and year.

Figures are the published values for the years shown; state income figures are rounded to the nearest $1,000. This site is a demonstration atlas and is not affiliated with the U.S. Government.
